**Startup Fundraising (Brad Flora, YC Partner)**

- **Fundraising is a Grind, Not a Show:** Real fundraising isn't a high-pressure "Shark Tank" event; it is a repetitive series of one-on-one coffee chats or Zoom calls that can require hundreds of meetings to close.

- **Build Before You Raise:** You do not need money to start. Building a prototype and getting early users first gives you the leverage needed to attract investors, who prefer "trains already in motion."

- **Convince Rather Than Impress:** You don't need "magic words" or a fancy pedigree; you simply need to explain in plain language how you are making something people want and how it has a chance to become huge.

- **The Power of the SAFE:** Modern fundraising is fast and cheap thanks to the SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity), a five-page document that allows founders to close deals in days without expensive legal fees.

- **Retain Founder Control:** Raising a seed round via SAFEs allows founders to keep total control of their company, as these agreements typically do not grant board seats or immediate information rights.

- **The Pitfalls of Bootstrapping:** While bootstrapping is an option, it is often a "distracting and scary" way to live; raising early capital can "rip off the Band-Aid" and allow you to focus entirely on the product.

- **Results Over Network:** Investors are "coin-operated" and care more about potential profit than your social network or where you went to school. If the product shows traction, the network doesn't matter.

- **Pitch Your Own Company:** Never hire someone else to raise money for you. Investors want to build a relationship directly with the founders, and you must own that connection.

- **Rejection is Normal:** Being turned down does not mean your startup is bad. Many multi-billion dollar companies (like Whatnot and Envision) were rejected dozens of times before finding their first believer.

- **Accessibility:** The ultimate myth is that "fundraising isn't for you." In reality, there has never been a better or more accessible time in history to raise money and build a startup.
