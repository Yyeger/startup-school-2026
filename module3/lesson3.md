**Co-Founder Equity Distribution**

- **Primary Goal of Equity:** The main objective of an equity split should be to maximize long-term motivation. It is the primary tool to ensure co-founders remain committed through the many years required to build a high-impact company.

- **The CEO's Responsibility:** A CEO should not just settle for what was "negotiated." They must proactively determine a split that ensures co-founders feel like true owners, even if those co-founders aren't currently thinking about their own long-term interests.

- **Safety via Vesting and Cliffs:** Instead of being stingy with equity to "protect" the company, use standard legal mechanisms as a hedge:
  - **Four-Year Vesting:** Ensures equity is earned over a long period of service.
  - **One-Year Cliff:** If a co-founder leaves or is fired within the first year, they receive zero equity. This serves as a "get out of jail free card" for bad hiring decisions.

- **Lean Toward Generosity:** Because vesting and cliffs provide a safety net, CEOs should be more generous rather than less. Generosity fosters an "owner mindset" (working weekends, recruiting friends, deep commitment) rather than an "employee mindset."

- **Equal Splits as a Benchmark:** While equal equity splits are a good general rule of thumb, they are not mandatory. The focus should always remain on what will drive future motivation.

- **The Litmus Test for Co-Founders:** If you do not feel a partner is worth a generous equity grant, you should reconsider whether they should be a co-founder at all.
