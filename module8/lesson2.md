**Anne Wojcicki - CEO, 23andMe**

## Professional Background & Founding Inspiration

- **Wall Street Experience:** Spent 10 years as a healthcare investor, gaining a broad landscape of biotech, pharma, and hospital systems.
- **The "Incentive Problem":** Identified that the current healthcare system effectively "monetizes illness" rather than incentivizing wellness.
- **The Catalyst:** Recognized a convergence of two major trends: the plummeting cost of genetic scanning and the rise of social networking/crowdsourcing.
- **Mission:** To create a "rebellious" company that empowers individuals to take control of their own health data from outside the traditional medical establishment.

## Strategic Business Decisions

- **Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Model:** Purposely bypassed doctors and insurance companies to put the consumer in the "power seat" of their own healthcare decisions.
- **Crowdsourced Research:** Built a "We the People" research model, allowing the company to aggregate massive amounts of data to solve diseases rather than relying solely on traditional academic institutions.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Prioritized hiring scientists with high humility and integrity; utilized peer-reviewed publications as the primary tool for gaining credibility in the medical community.

## Overcoming Operational Challenges

- **The "Trough of Sorrow":** After an initial high-profile launch (including "spit parties"), the company faced slow growth (15-25 kits a day).
- **Educational Pivot:** Realized consumers didn't understand the value of genetics; shifted strategy to focus on massive public education and personalizing the "utility" of the data.
- **Regulatory Transition:** Successfully navigated the move from an unregulated startup to a regulated entity, learning to work with the FDA by speaking the "language of data" and respecting public safety mandates.

## Leadership & Growth Philosophy

- **The Power of Persistence:** Wojcicki emphasizes that "overnight success" took 10+ years of sticking to the same original vision and series-A goals.
- **Hiring Framework:** Evaluates candidates based on their "net output" on the organization, favoring those who are open to feedback over those who simply want to prove their intelligence.
- **Vision-Led Management:** Maintains momentum by focusing the team on a two-to-five-year plan rather than wallowing in daily setbacks.

## Future Roadmap (The Next 11 Years)

- **The "Discovery Machine":** Leveraging the world's largest genetic dataset (5M+ customers) for drug discovery.
- **Intervention Studies:** Moving from providing "risk data" to conducting large-scale studies (e.g., on weight loss and Parkinson's) to tell people exactly what behaviors will keep them healthy.
- **The Ultimate Goal:** Shifting the healthcare focus toward "prevention" so individuals can remain healthy at 100 years old.
