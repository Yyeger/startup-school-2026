**Mark Zuckerberg - Founder & CEO, Facebook**

## Professional Experience

**Facebook | Founder & CEO | 2004 – Present**

### Product Innovation & Scaling:
- Launched the initial version of Facebook as a campus directory at Harvard to solve the "thirst for understanding" social connections; grew to 2/3 of the student body within weeks.
- Led the company through its transition from a college-only service to a global platform, reaching 10 million users within the first two years.
- Spearheaded the development and launch of News Feed, shifting the platform from static profiles to a real-time stream of updates.
- Pioneered the concept of the "Growth Group," utilizing data-driven engineering (e.g., "People You May Know") rather than traditional marketing to scale the user base.

### Strategic Leadership & Decision Making:
- Successfully navigated the "Yahoo Crisis" (2006) by rejecting a $1 billion acquisition offer to preserve the company's long-term mission of connecting the world.
- Managed a total leadership turnover following the Yahoo offer, rebuilding the management team from the ground up with a focus on mission alignment.
- Orchestrated major strategic bets on the future of computing, including the acquisition of Oculus to lead the VR/AR industry.

### Operational Philosophy:
- Implemented a "Scientific Method" approach to business, running tens of thousands of simultaneous versions of Facebook to learn and iterate based on real-time data.
- Established a culture of empowering individual engineers to ship experiments to hundreds of thousands of users without top-down approval.

## Core Skills & Competencies

- **Talent Identification:** Expert at assessing "raw talent" over specific experience; maintains a 90%+ internal promotion rate for top product leadership.
- **Strategic Roadmapping:** Designed a 10-year vision focused on three pillars: Global Connectivity, Artificial Intelligence, and the next computing platform (VR/AR).
- **Mission-Driven Product Design:** Philosophy centered on starting with a social problem to solve rather than the desire to build a company.
- **Risk Management:** Deep belief in the philosophy that "in a world changing quickly, the biggest risk is not taking any risk."

## Education

**Harvard University | Psychology & Computer Science**

- Developed "CourseMatch" and multiple crowdsourced tools to understand human behavior and social correlations prior to launching Facebook.
- **Note:** Departed to pursue Facebook full-time.

## Key Initiatives & Future Vision

- **Connectivity:** Committed to bringing the internet to the 50% of the global population currently without access.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Investing in AI to improve medical diagnostics (e.g., skin cancer detection), road safety, and personalized user experiences.
- **The Next Platform:** Developing Virtual and Augmented Reality as the successor to mobile computing to enable more immersive human connection.
